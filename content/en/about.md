{{< caption >}}Murcia, Spain. In a lab full of cables, half-finished coffees and toys on the floor…{{< /caption >}}

{{< bubble >}}Hi! I'm **José Francisco Rives**, though online I've been signing as **SeViR** for half my life.{{< /bubble >}}

Above all, I'm a software inventor. As **CTO at [DIGIO](https://www.digio.es)**, a digital product studio in Murcia with more than 30 people, I take care of the technical architecture, make sure teams have good tools and mentor younger developers, who now work side by side with AI agents.

Almost twenty years ago I designed, from the very first line, a **SaaS platform** that is still in production, growing and moving to new technology. I know every corner of that invention, and it has taught me more about keeping software alive than any book.

## What I do now

- **AI agents and MCP servers**: [Pando](https://github.com/digiogithub/pando), [Remembrances](https://github.com/madeindigio/remembrances-mcp), Mesnada and a collection of tools that help agents work better.
- **Rust and Go tools**: single, fast, dependency-free binaries such as the [MicroESP](https://github.com/sevir/microesp) agent.
- **Product and architecture**: TypeScript, Python, modern PHP and migrating systems with a long history.

## What I used to do

Part-time sysadmin, CodeIgniter-era PHP developer, author of the [SeViR Notes](/blog/) blog (in Spanish), handyman at home and skating instructor. Some things never change.
